# Privacy Policy for Cash On Hand

*Last updated: [Current Date]*

## Overview

Cash On Hand is a personal financial management app that helps you track income, expenses, and savings goals. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your financial data remains secure.

## Data Collection and Storage

### What We Collect
- **Financial Transactions**: Income and expense records you manually enter
- **Savings Goals**: Goal targets and progress you create  
- **Categories**: Transaction categorization data
- **App Preferences**: Settings and UI preferences

### What We DON'T Collect
- **No Personal Identity Information**: We don't collect names, emails, or personal identifiers
- **No Banking Information**: We don't connect to banks or financial institutions
- **No Location Data**: We don't track your location
- **No Analytics**: We don't use tracking or analytics services

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- All your financial data is stored **locally on your device**
- Your data **never leaves your device** or gets transmitted to our servers
- We don't have servers or cloud storage - everything stays with you

### Data Security
- Financial data uses device-level encryption
- No network connections are required for app functionality
- Your data is protected by your device's security measures

## Data Sharing

**We do not share your data with anyone** because:
- We don't collect your data on our servers
- We don't have access to your financial information
- The app works completely offline

## Your Rights

### Data Control
- **Full Ownership**: You own all your financial data
- **Complete Control**: You can export or delete your data anytime
- **No Vendor Lock-in**: Your data remains accessible to you

### Data Portability
- You can export your data from the app settings
- Data is stored in standard formats for easy migration

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted in the app.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us through the app store review system.

## Compliance

This app complies with:
- Apple App Store privacy requirements
- Google Play Store privacy requirements  
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) principles

---

**Key Promise**: Your financial data stays on your device. We never see it, store it, or share it.
